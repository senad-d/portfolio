## Problem

Years of cloud and DevOps work produced ideas for products and services that did not fit inside client engagements, and shipping them needed a legal home, a public face, and honest product descriptions.

## Approach

Set up Micky as a Croatian sole-trader studio built around modern AI tooling and production-grade engineering discipline. Positioned it on three interconnected areas: native Android apps that stay fast and private, platform work covering serverless backends, secure AWS foundations, and automated delivery with observability, and AI integration covering language-model features, agent tooling with guardrails, and data-to-decision pipelines. Built the studio site with Astro, Three.js, GSAP, and Lenis and deployed it to Cloudflare Pages.

## Result

Micky is live with CamLock as its first product and a clear service offer. Its stated mission is Machine Intelligence for Cognitive Knowledge Yield, and every product ships with plain-language privacy practices.
